Timotheos 0.6.121

- New: Translation Accuracy Rating (TAR). The TAR button of the Parallel Bibles panel (or View > Translation Accuracy Rating) asks an AI model of your choice to grade how faithfully a translation renders its source text (for example the Textus Receptus or the LXX), verse by verse.
- Five measures: word meaning 30%, grammar 25%, completeness 20%, meaning and emphasis 15%, consistency 10%. Grades: Poor 0-59, Not Bad 60-79, Good 80-91, Faithful 92-100.
- Rate one verse, the chapter, the book, or the entire translation. Judge as word for word or thought for thought; notes in English or Tagalog. Differences that come from a different source text are not counted. Stop at any time; a new start goes on where it stopped.
- Results are saved per Bible. Each rated verse shows its grade in the Parallel Bibles panel; click it for the five measures, the AI's note and the words that lose something. The TAR dialog shows the saved averages for everything and per book.
- AI settings: Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Groq, Mistral, xAI, a model on this computer (Ollama, LM Studio), or any OpenAI-compatible address. Your key stays on this device only and is never synced. List models and Test buttons.
- User Guide updated (computer and phone).
