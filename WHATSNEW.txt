Timotheos 0.6.36
- Downloads (updates and modules) no longer fail with "Dns Failed" until the app is restarted. When the phone cannot look up github.com (for example after a VPN was switched on or off while Timotheos was open), Timotheos tries again and then asks a public DNS service (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1, then Google 8.8.8.8) for the address. Only the name of the site is sent there.
