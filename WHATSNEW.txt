Timotheos 0.6.65
- SWORD commentaries, dictionaries and general books: the CrossWire packages for these now import too (File > Import Modules, or drag them onto the window). Commentaries show in the Commentary panel for the verse you read; dictionaries and lexicons, including Strong's Greek and Hebrew, work in the Library and in the word pop-up; general books show as a book with its sections.
- Tested against real SWORD modules (KJV with Strong's, Luther, Matthew Henry, Treasury of David, Nave's Topical Bible, Strong's dictionaries) and against modules written by SWORD's own programs in all its numbering systems and compressions.
- SWORD Bibles: a module whose verse index stops a record or two early, or whose record size differs from its driver name, is now read correctly; Strong's numbers written as savlm are recognised.
- A SWORD module's .conf shows in its properties (read only).
- User Guide updated.
