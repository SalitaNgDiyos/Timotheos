Timotheos 0.6.58
- Desktop: drag the text to scroll it. Hold the left mouse button on a Bible view, commentary, library entry, search results, My Study or the Bible tree and drag; hold Shift to select text instead; the middle mouse button always drags. Option: Options > Layout & toolbar > "Drag the text to scroll it".
- Desktop: a click anywhere in a verse selects it, including the blank space after it and between its lines.
- Linux: text no longer turns blurry while scrolling (software compositing is now the default; WEBKIT_DISABLE_COMPOSITING_MODE=0 restores the old way). This was in 0.6.57.
- User Guide updated for all of the above.
