Timotheos 0.6.60
- Word pop-ups per Bible view: a new button (a little dictionary) in the left icon bar of each Bible view controls the pop-up of tagged words (Strong's number, morphology, dictionary entry).
- It is off at first: hold Shift and point at a word to see the pop-up. Click the button to have it open by itself in that view; click again to turn it off. Each view remembers its own setting.
- Footnote and cross-reference pop-ups and verse-link previews are not affected.
- The tooltip of the button explains it; the User Guide has the steps.
- (0.6.59 hand buttons for drag-to-scroll are included.)
