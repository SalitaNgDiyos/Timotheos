Timotheos 0.6.97

- Persistent Panels dialog: clicking the Panels button now opens a floating, movable dialog box with an X close button that stays open until you close it, letting you drag and drop multiple panels in a row whenever you create empty spaces without clicking Panels again.
- Immediate warning dismissal: when the Panels dialog is open and you open an empty space (at least 1 inch by 1 inch) by resizing or moving panels, the no-space warning disappears immediately and all panel kinds become draggable live.
