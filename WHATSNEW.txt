Timotheos 0.6.131
- Updated the bundled Ang Malayang Biblia (AMB) module to the 2026.1 edition with the updated About page.
- Real-time AMB Bible auto-update: on startup on both PC and Android, Timotheos now checks for live updates published to Ang-Malayang-Biblia-Translation, updates your installed AMB Bible silently in-place, and refreshes the open Bible view immediately.
- USFM 3.0 archives (.usfm.zip) and folders now load their embedded About page (copr.htm / about.htm / README.html).


- Look up by right-click: a click in the Bible text no longer opens the Library. Right-click a word → Look up (on a Strong's word with its Strong's entries; on a footnote or cross-reference mark: Show … in the Library). Options → "Click a word to look it up in the Library" (off at first) brings the click back. The phone is unchanged.
- One panel per space: panels are never tabs any more. Panels that were tabbed together are split side by side; floating windows hold one panel each. The Compact layout (all tabs) is gone.
- Opening a closed panel (a lookup, Bible Search, a book link…) first asks "Add the … panel?" with a map of the window: it suggests splitting the panel you are using; point at another panel and side, or an empty space, then Add panel. Cancel leaves everything as it was.
- Updates and module downloads: when Timotheos' own download stalls or fails, it tries again by itself with the computer's own downloader (curl, on Linux and Windows 10/11); the file is still checked against GitHub's fingerprint before installing.
- Right-click menu labels are clearer: Find “word” in this Bible, Find G1385 in this Bible, and Look up “word” in Library.
- A closed panel (or any empty space) now shows two links: Add Panel (pick any panel to fill that space) and Split Space (divide it; only offered while the space is big enough). They work with Edit Layout on or off.
- Phone: the buttons in the verse sheet (hold a verse) now work with one tap; the first tap was being swallowed after the hold.
- Edit Layout turns off when the Layout & Panels window is closed. The drag grip is removed from panel headers. The number of chapters per book is no longer shown in the books list and pickers. The phone app no longer has the Clipboard monitor.
