Timotheos 0.6.43
- Reading aloud on the phone is rebuilt. It is now done by a background service, not by the page, so it no longer stops and starts verse by verse: the next verses are queued ahead and there are no gaps or cut-offs between them.
- It keeps going when the screen is locked or the app is in the background. A player with previous verse, pause, next verse and stop shows in the notification and on the lock screen. (The first time, Android asks to allow notifications.)
- The player in the app stays on screen the whole time something is being read, even when the text is scrolled (it used to slide away).
- Battery saver: the first time you read aloud, Android asks whether Timotheos may run in the background without battery restrictions; choose Allow. The same button is in Options → Read aloud → Background playback. Some phones (Xiaomi/Redmi, for example) also have their own per-app battery setting that only you can change: Settings → Apps → Timotheos → Battery saver → No restrictions.
- When you open the app again while it is reading, it picks up where the reading is, marks the verse and shows the player.
- The next chapters (four ahead) are lined up in advance, so reading on into the next chapter also works with the screen off.
