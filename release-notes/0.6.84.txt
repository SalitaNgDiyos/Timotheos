Timotheos 0.6.84
- Verse link popups: added multi-Bible selection in Options > Modules > Verse links (popups and previews) for Old Testament, New Testament, and a new Deuterocanon / Apocrypha group (filtered to installed Bibles that contain Deuterocanon books).
- Parallel hover preview: when two or more Bibles are ticked for a group, hovering a verse link in books, dictionaries, commentaries, or cross-references opens those Bibles side by side in a parallel popup (stacking automatically on narrow screens).
- Legacy Deuterocanon links: hovering or clicking legacy theWord Deuterocanon book links (such as DeuteroKJV links to Sirach, Wisdom, Tobit, or Maccabees) now resolves to your selected Deuterocanon Bibles for parallel preview and navigation.
