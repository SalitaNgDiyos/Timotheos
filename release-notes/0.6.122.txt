Timotheos 0.6.122

- Detach edges mode: closing a panel now leaves an empty space where it was, and no other panel moves or grows. This also holds for Bible views: closing one Bible view leaves its own place empty and the other views stay where they are (before, the views after it shifted into its place).
- New: undo and redo arrows beside the Help menu for layout changes (closing, opening, moving and resizing panels, layouts chosen, Bible views closed; a closed Bible view comes back with its Bible). Also View > Undo / Redo Layout Change, and Ctrl+Alt+Z / Ctrl+Alt+Y (changeable in Options > Shortcuts).
- User Guide updated.
