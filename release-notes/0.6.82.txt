Timotheos 0.6.82
- Panel layout: added a "Detach edges" toggle button (toolbar, Panels menu, and Options → Layout & toolbar) to shrink panel edges independently without stretching neighbours and keep emptied spaces open while arranging.
- Panel docking: dragging a panel now shows smart drop zones on every empty space you created plus Left / Right / Top / Bottom / Tab zones on panels and outer window edges, with exact live drop previews.
- 4×3 snap grid: fixed the optional 4×3 snap grid toggle so cells accurately fill empty or partially empty resized spaces, split spanning panels, and tab onto single-cell panels.
- Layout thumbnails: Options → Layout & toolbar and the Panels menu now show real miniature SVG thumbnails of each built-in and saved layout instead of generic icons.
