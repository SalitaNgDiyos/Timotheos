Timotheos 0.6.64
- SWORD Bibles: Bibles from The SWORD Project (CrossWire) open in Timotheos. Import the Raw ZIP package you download from crosswire.org (File > Import Modules, or drag it onto the window). The zText, zText4, RawText and RawText4 types are read, compressed with zlib, bzip2 or xz, with Strong's numbers, morphology, footnotes, cross-references and the words of Jesus.
- Locked SWORD modules (CipherKey) and very old LZSS modules are skipped with a clear message.
- Bibles whose verse numbering differs from the King James numbering show their own numbers, with a note under the module list.
- SWORD commentaries, dictionaries, general books and a download list inside Timotheos are not in this version yet.
- User Guide updated.
