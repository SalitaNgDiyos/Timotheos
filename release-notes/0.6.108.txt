Timotheos 0.6.108

- Pop-out multi-monitor Panels & Layouts window: click the pop-out button in the Panels & Layouts dialog header to open it as a native OS window that you can drag anywhere across your monitor or onto a second monitor.
- Close all panels & Design your layout: you can now close every panel (including the last Bible view, or via Close all panels in Panels & Layouts) to reveal a centered "Design your layout" button.
- Word-style animated 4x3 grid designer with adjustable dotted edges: clicking "Design your layout" opens an interactive 1x1 to 4x3 table-style grid picker; confirming displays dotted blueprint lines whose edges you can drag to resize before dragging panels from Panels & Layouts into each slot.
